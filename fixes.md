# Tone problems and fixes

Gemini picks problem ids from this list. The report shows the fix for the top 1–2 problems, copied word for word.
Keep fixes in simple words, with one example line each.

## same-shape
**Problem:** Every chunk has the same melody (usually up ↗ at the end), so it sounds like reading a list.
**Fix:**
1. Hit one word per chunk. Say it a bit louder and higher. Say the rest fast and light.
   "I break AI down in SIMPLE words"
2. End statements down ↘. Like putting a cup on the table, don't let it float up.
   "And that's completely FINE ↘."
3. Lists go up, up, down. "You open it ↗, you type ↗, you ask questions ↘."

## no-punch
**Problem:** No word stands out. Every word gets the same weight.
**Fix:**
1. Underline ONE word per chunk: the word that carries the meaning (a number, a name, a feeling).
2. Say that word slower and louder, like you're leaning on it.
   "This saved me TWO hours"
3. Say the small words (the, a, and, to, of) fast and quiet.

## upspeak
**Problem:** Statements end going up ↗, so they sound like questions or like you're unsure.
**Fix:**
1. On the last word of a statement, let your voice drop ↘ and stop.
   "This is the BEST way ↘."
2. Only go up ↗ when you really ask something, or to pull people to the next line.
3. Practice: say the sentence, then nod once on the last word. The nod pulls your voice down.

## monotone
**Problem:** Your voice stays on almost one note, so it sounds flat or bored.
**Fix:**
1. Exaggerate: read your hook like you're telling a 5-year-old a story, then film at half that.
2. Go higher on good news and surprise, lower on serious points.
   "And it was FREE ↗ … but there's ONE catch ↘"
3. Smile on the first line. It lifts your voice by itself.

## hurrying
**Problem:** You talk too fast with no breaks, so nothing lands.
**Fix:**
1. Cut long sentences into chunks of 2–4 words, with a tiny breath between them.
   "So here's the thing / most people / do this WRONG"
2. Pause for one beat right BEFORE the important word.
3. Slow down on the key point, speed up on the easy parts.

## dragging
**Problem:** Too slow, or long pauses in the wrong places, so energy drops.
**Fix:**
1. Say the easy connecting parts faster: "and then", "so basically", "what you do is".
2. Pause only before or after a key word, not in the middle of a thought.
3. Imagine you're telling a friend something exciting, not reading it.

## trailing-off
**Problem:** The ends of sentences get quiet or mumbled, so the last words get lost.
**Fix:**
1. Keep your volume up through the LAST word, then stop.
2. Breathe in before a sentence, not in the middle of it.
3. Practice: say the last word of each sentence a little louder than feels normal.

## low-energy
**Problem:** Clear but flat energy, so it sounds tired or careful.
**Fix:**
1. Stand up or sit forward. Use your hands.
2. Start the first line at a higher energy than feels comfortable. The camera eats about 30% of it.
3. Think of one person you're talking to and why they need this.

## unclear
**Problem:** Some words are hard to catch (mumbled sounds, swallowed endings).
**Fix:**
1. Open your mouth more on the key words.
2. Finish the last sound of each word ("worked", "next", "best").
3. Slow down slightly on names of tools and numbers.
